1. Page Structure — Section Reorder

The page section order was restructured to follow a SAR copywriting framework (Situation → Agitate → Resolve). High-impact content now appears earlier, while the page holds trust and pricing until after the visitor understands the value.

Pending Decisions — Executive Team

These items must be resolved before the page goes live.

ItemDecision RequiredImpact
Favicon Asset does not exist. Needs to be created and supplied. Missing favicon looks unprofessional in browser tabs and bookmarks
Logo Final approved version needs sign-off before go-live. Cannot publish without confirmed brand asset
£100 Credit vs Free Trial Page consistently references £100 Free Credit. Confirm this is the live offer. Hero CTA, pricing section, and FAQ all reference it. Inconsistency between offer types at launch damages trust
Pricing model Current per-page volume band model requires visitors to calculate their own monthly cost — adds friction. Consider a 3-tier flat monthly structure (Starter / Growth / Scale) as used by Dext and Hubdoc. Conversion rate risk. Recommend resolving before launch if possible.
Demo booking "Book a 15-Min Demo" CTA button currently links to #. Needs a Calendly or Microsoft Bookings URL. Recommend Calendly (free tier available, Google & Outlook compatible). CTA button non-functional at launch
Social media LinkedIn and Twitter removed from footer — no active accounts. Confirm whether accounts will be created for launch. Absence of social presence may affect trust signals
Customer quotes Confirm all three testimonials are from real customers and that written consent has been obtained for use of full names and quotes publicly. Legal risk if quotes are illustrative and not confirmed
